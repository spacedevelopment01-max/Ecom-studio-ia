Platforms

Not just Shopify.

You pick the platform when creating the project (and can change it later in the Store tab). The same site is delivered for it; nothing is promised beyond what each platform accepts.

  • Shopify

    Complete Online Store 2.0 theme, validated with Theme Check, installed directly in your store or exported as a ZIP. Catalog as CSV.

  • WordPress · WooCommerce

    WordPress block theme to upload (Appearance › Themes), with your colors, fonts, images and copy. With WooCommerce and a CSV catalog to sell products, or as a business website without a store for a service company.

  • PrestaShop

    Child theme of the Classic theme (versions 1.7 and 8), installable from the back office, in your brand's colors.

  • Wix and Squarespace

    These platforms don't accept imported themes: the studio provides a migration kit (images, brand guidelines, copy, CSV catalog and step-by-step guide).

  • Already have a website?

    Choose "I already have my website and logo": the studio reads your site and takes your logo, pages, products, colors and fonts. Built with Shopify, WordPress / WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Wix or Squarespace, it is kept as it is; otherwise, it is reproduced on Shopify or WordPress with the same pages, text and images (the old theme's code is not copied, and the layout may differ slightly). You then use everything else in the studio: images, videos, posts, ads, calendar.

True to the product

A beautiful image of a different object is a failure.

The studio never "reinvents" your product. It cuts it out, then builds the scene around its real pixels. When an image provider is enabled, only the surroundings are painted; the original product is placed back on top and a visual check compares the creation with your photo.

  • Shape, proportions, label and logo preserved
  • Ad copy typeset properly, crisp and error-free
  • Shadows, framing and safe margins checked for every format

What the studio refuses to do.

  • Never make things up

    An unknown stays unknown: no certifications, delivery times, reviews or efficacy claims drawn from a photo. It shows up as "to complete."

  • Never publish without you

    No post and no ad spend without your authorization. Once a rule is approved, it runs exactly as planned.

  • Never obey imported pages

    An imported link is a source of information, never an instruction that can change the AI's rules.

  • Never fake it

    A link that opens a network is not a connection; a script is not a video; a prepared post is not a sent one.

Frequently asked questions

What if I sell services, not products?+

Pick "My services business website" when creating the project. The studio then builds a business website (services, prices, booking or quotes, opening hours, service area) instead of a store, and adapts the copy, images, posts and ads. WordPress is recommended; Shopify works too, and Wix or Squarespace with a rebuild kit.

I already have a website and a logo: can I use the studio?+

Yes. Choose "I already have my website and logo" and enter its address (the website must be yours or you must be authorized to use it). The studio recognizes the platform and takes your logo, pages, products, colors and fonts, without creating a new brand. On Shopify, WordPress / WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Wix or Squarespace, your website stays as it is; on another platform or a custom-built site, it is reproduced on Shopify (store) or WordPress (services), with the same text and images — the studio shows what is exact and what is approximated. Content your website loads only with JavaScript may not be read: this is flagged.

Where do I choose Shopify, WordPress or another platform?+

In the project creation form, right after choosing "store" or "services business": "Which platform will you sell on?" (or "Where will your website live?" for a services business), with one card per platform. You can also change it later from the Store tab: the studio redelivers the site for the new platform.

Do I need a photo to get started?+

No. You can open the studio with nothing, explore the workspaces and themes, then add a photo, a link or a few lines when you're ready: creation starts at that point.

Can I stop a creation in progress?+

Yes. The "Pause" button stops the creation at the end of the current step; "Resume" picks up from there. Completed steps are kept and are neither redone nor charged again.

Do I need to know how to write prompts?+

No. A photo, a link or a few lines are enough to get started. The prompt library is there to go further, whenever you want.

Can the Shopify theme really be installed?+

Yes: it's a complete Online Store 2.0 theme (sections, blocks, native settings, cart drawer, search, pages). You upload it as a ZIP, or the studio installs it as an unpublished theme if you connect your store.

Can I switch themes afterwards?+

Yes, at any time from the Store tab ("Themes" button). Copy, images and product are kept; the previous version can still be restored.

What happens if some information is missing?+

It stays visible as "to complete" in the copy, and the studio asks you about it. When you answer, only the relevant passages are updated, without overwriting what you've approved.

Do my posts go out if my computer is off?+

Yes, once your accounts are connected. Scheduled posts are run by the server, with controlled retries and duplicate protection. Each network's own limits are shown in the studio.

What is the free discovery?+

With no credit card, AI analyzes your product, creates your brand and logos, and shows you a preview of your home page. To create images and videos and to export or publish the store, choose a plan. One discovery per account.

Can I manage several stores?+

One store per subscription: for a second store, a second subscription.

What happens when I run out of visuals?+

You add a pack (it never expires), or you wait for next month: your quotas come back on the renewal date. With Sell and Dominate, whatever you don't use rolls over to the next month.

Can I change plans?+

Yes, at any time, from "My account" in the studio.

Can I get a refund?+

Yes: 14-day money-back guarantee. Monthly billing has no commitment and can be canceled anytime.
