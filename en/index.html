FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Another question? Write to us.

What if I sell services, not products?+

Pick "My services business website" when creating the project. The studio then builds a business website (services, prices, booking or quotes, opening hours, service area) instead of a store, and adapts the copy, images, posts and ads. WordPress is recommended; Shopify works too, and Wix or Squarespace with a rebuild kit.

I already have a website and a logo: can I use the studio?+

Yes. Choose "I already have my website and logo" and enter its address (the website must be yours or you must be authorized to use it). The studio recognizes the platform and takes your logo, pages, products, colors and fonts, without creating a new brand. On Shopify, WordPress / WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Wix or Squarespace, your website stays as it is; on another platform or a custom-built site, it is reproduced on Shopify (store) or WordPress (services), with the same text and images — the studio shows what is exact and what is approximated. Content your website loads only with JavaScript may not be read: this is flagged.

Where do I choose Shopify, WordPress or another platform?+

In the project creation form, right after choosing "store" or "services business": "Which platform will you sell on?" (or "Where will your website live?" for a services business), with one card per platform. You can also change it later from the Store tab: the studio redelivers the site for the new platform.

Do I need a photo to get started?+

No. You can open the studio with nothing, explore the workspaces and themes, then add a photo, a link or a few lines when you're ready: creation starts at that point.

Are the demos on this page client results?+

No. They are brands created by the studio, without the plans' AI, from products sold white-label by suppliers (plus a fictional services website). The small interactive demos run in your browser: nothing is saved or published.

Can I stop a creation in progress?+

Yes. The "Pause" button stops the creation at the end of the current step; "Resume" picks up from there. Completed steps are kept and are neither redone nor charged again.

Can the Shopify theme really be installed?+

Yes: it's a complete Online Store 2.0 theme (sections, blocks, native settings, cart drawer, search, pages), checked by Theme Check, Shopify's official tool. You upload it as a ZIP. Direct upload to your store is built but not yet tried on a real store.

What happens if some information is missing?+

It stays visible as "to complete" in the copy, and the studio asks you about it. When you answer, only the relevant passages are updated, without overwriting what you've approved.

Do my posts go out on their own?+

Only the ones you've approved, once your accounts are connected. Publishing through the official connections (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest) is built but not yet tried on real accounts; for LinkedIn, the studio prepares posts for you to publish yourself.

What is the free discovery?+

With no credit card, the studio engine analyzes your product, creates your brand and logos, and shows you a preview of your home page. The discovery doesn't use AI: to get AI, create images and videos, and export or publish the store, choose a plan. One discovery per account.

Can I manage several stores?+

One store per subscription: for a second store, a second subscription.

What happens when I run out of visuals?+

You add a pack (it never expires), or you wait for next month: your quotas come back on the renewal date. With Sell and Dominate, whatever you don't use rolls over to the next month.

Can I change plans?+

Yes, at any time, from "My account" in the studio.

Can I get a refund?+

Yes: 14-day money-back guarantee. Monthly billing has no commitment and can be canceled anytime.
