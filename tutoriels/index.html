Connexions 1:16

Relier vos comptes (réseaux sociaux, Canva, Shopify) pour publier et installer depuis le studio, sans jamais donner votre mot de passe.

See all your posts on a calendar, move them to another day and prepare several days of posts in advance.

  1. The month calendar: each post sits on its day, with its time. The colored dot shows the network.
  2. The arrows change the month; "Today" brings you back to the current date.
  3. Switch to "Week" or "Day" view for more detail. Clicking a day number opens that day.
  4. Click a post to open and edit it, just like in the Posts tab.
  5. To change its day, drag it onto another square: it keeps its time. A post that has been sent can't be moved.
  6. At the bottom, the list of possible statuses: draft, to review, scheduled, published, failed…
  7. "Prepare posts": choose the first day, the number of days, how many per day and at what times.
  8. On the right, tick the networks and their accounts, then set the share of photos, videos and text. The posts then arrive "to review".
  9. "Automation rules" lets you allow scheduling without approval, network by network. Otherwise, you approve everything yourself.

Ads 1:16

Prepare your ad campaigns (angles, copy, creatives), then export them to each network's ads manager.

  1. The studio prepares your campaigns but spends nothing: budget and launch are set in each network's ads manager.
  2. "New campaign" opens the campaign form. The angles from your brand strategy are suggested as a starting point.
  3. Name the campaign, choose the objective and tick the networks you want.
  4. Describe the audience and the metrics you will track. The audience is also used to write the ads.
  5. Choose the language, then "Draft ads in this language". Always review the suggested copy.
  6. Each ad has an angle, a primary text, a headline and a button. The "+" adds your images or videos.
  7. "Save" keeps the campaign. "Create organic posts" also turns it into drafts in Posts.
  8. The campaign shows up in the list. "Export for the ads manager" downloads all the ads as a CSV file.

Files 1:15

Find, open, organize and upload every file in the project: photos, images, videos, logos and exports.

  1. All the project's files are here. On the left, the folders: the studio files each of its creations there by itself.
  2. Click a folder to open it. The path at the top lets you go back up a level.
  3. Click a file to open it: dimensions, source, and the "Approve", "Download" or "Place" in the store actions.
  4. Tick files to move them or send them to the trash. You can also drag them onto a folder on the left.
  5. Switch from thumbnails to the list: it shows each file's type, status, size and date.
  6. "Search everywhere" finds a file in every folder, by its name or type.
  7. "Upload" adds your own files to the open folder. You can also drag them straight onto the page.
  8. "New subfolder" creates a folder inside the open one. "Sort unfiled files" files away the stray ones, without deleting anything.
  9. The "Trash" keeps deleted files: you can always restore them.

Connections 1:13

Link your accounts (social networks, Canva, Shopify) to publish and install from the studio, without ever giving your password.

  1. This is where you link your accounts. No password is asked: each platform gives its official authorization.
  2. Each card shows a platform and what it allows. Meta publishes to your Facebook Page and your Instagram professional account.
  3. "API limits and requested permissions" details what the platform allows, with a link to its official documentation.
  4. The badge shows the status: "Available" means you can connect; "Not configured" means the studio administrator must enable the platform first.
  5. When the platform is available, the "Connect" button appears here. It opens its authorization page, then brings you back to the studio.
  6. TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest publish your videos and pins. Canva receives your creations for editing, then sends them back to the studio.
  7. Shopify installs your theme as an unpublished theme, and creates your store's product and pages.
  8. CapCut has no public API: the studio prepares a pack to import, from the Videos or Files areas.
